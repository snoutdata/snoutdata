# Plans and billing

SnoutData has four plans: **Free**, **Plus**, **Pro** and **Business**. The app and your
database features are available on every plan; the plans differ mainly in AI usage, a few
assistant features, and, on Business, single sign-on for a team.

## What a plan unlocks

- **Free** gives you the full IDE, every supported database, plus a starter amount of AI usage.
- **Plus** and **Pro** add more AI usage and unlock [Bring Your Own Key](../ai-assistant/byok),
  letting you use your own AI provider account.
- **Business** is Pro for every seat on one bill, plus SAML single sign-on with your own
  identity provider. It is sold by the seat, from five seats.

The exact allowances and prices are on the [pricing page](https://snoutdata.com/?view=account).

![The account settings panel: plan badge, AI usage for the billing period, and credits](/img/screenshots/accounts-plan-settings.png)

## Managing your plan

Sign in on [snoutdata.com](https://snoutdata.com) to buy, change, or cancel a plan. Billing
is handled by Stripe; the app picks up your new plan automatically on its next refresh, so
there is nothing to re-enter in the desktop app.

## Signing in

The desktop app signs in with the same account as the website (Google or GitHub). Your plan
follows your account across both.
